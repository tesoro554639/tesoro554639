## 🐍 3. Enable the Snake Contribution Animation

Your profile includes a dynamic animation of a snake eating your contribution dots!
To activate the automatic daily generator:

1. Open your repository on GitHub: [https://github.com/tesoro554639/tesoro554639](https://github.com/tesoro554639/tesoro554639)
2. Click **Settings** (top tabs of the repository).
3. On the left menu, click **Actions** ➔ **General**.
4. Scroll down to **Workflow permissions**:
   - Select **"Read and write permissions"**.
   - Check the box for **"Allow GitHub Actions to approve pull requests"**.
   - Click **Save**.
5. Click the **Actions** tab at the top of the repository:
   - In the left sidebar, click **"Generate Contribution Snake Animation"**.
   - Click the **"Run workflow"** button on the right, and choose branch `main`.
6. Once it completes (usually ~45 seconds), it will automatically generate the dark snake SVG!
